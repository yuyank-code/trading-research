# Run 22 — validation literature and execution-alignment gate

Date: 2026-09-02

## Meaningful findings

1. Current trading repository remains structurally blocked from final economic evaluation by the open executable-event alignment issue (#7): supervised target is Close[t]→Close[t+6], while execution begins at Open[t+1]. This is not direct look-ahead by itself, but the prediction event and monetized event differ.
2. Issue #8 remains the correct next pipeline hardening step: represent label/event intervals explicitly and separate label purging from a post-test embargo.
3. Recent 2026 literature independently reinforces the project's cost-first design. Bysik & Ślepaczuk report that hourly BTC-USDT sign strategies fail at 10 bps while cost-aware forecast filtering can reduce turnover and restore profitability in selected configurations; model-family dominance was not formally established.
4. Kim (2026) reports a large disconnect between predictive metrics and economic value across 340 crypto ML variants, including strong apparent statistical results that fail CPCV/economic gates. This supports treating AUC/accuracy as diagnostic rather than a trading objective.
5. A 2026 live-trading case study reports a walk-forward model with AUC 0.85 becoming economically negligible live, highlighting execution-prediction mismatch and regime instability as practical failure modes. This is preprint evidence and should not be treated as settled fact, but it is directly relevant to our validation design.
6. Bailey et al.'s PBO/CSCV framework remains the primary guard against selecting a lucky backtest winner after searching many configurations. Trial count must include model, feature, threshold and execution-policy variants.

## Testable hypotheses for the next executable experiment

H1: Event-aligned labels materially change candidate ranking versus the current Close-to-Close target.
H2: Adding an explicit embargo reduces apparent OOS performance if serial dependence was contaminating selection; if it does not, robustness improves.
H3: A cost-aware abstention threshold should reduce turnover and improve net return/Sharpe at fixed forecast quality, but only if the improvement survives the predeclared cost grid.
H4: After DSR/PBO correction, the probability that the selected strategy retains positive risk-adjusted OOS performance should be materially lower than its raw best-backtest estimate.

## Gate

No profitability claim is promoted until: executable-event labels are frozen; event-time purge + embargo tests pass; OOS predictions are immutable; costs/slippage are applied without re-fitting; candidate selection is isolated from final holdout; and DSR/PBO/CSCV results are recorded.
