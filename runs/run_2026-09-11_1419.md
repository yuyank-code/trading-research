## Literature update

- Bailey & López de Prado (2014), *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio*, formalizes correction for multiple-testing selection bias and non-normal returns. https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2014.40.5.094
- Santoni, Jouanne & Scullin (2026), *Equity Strategy Backtesting: Luck or Edge?*, combines DSR, PBO, SPA, minimum track record length and regime stability as an auditable post-selection layer, while explicitly stating that the score is not evidence of future profitability.
- Huang, Wang & Jiang (2026), *Research on Machine Learning High-Frequency Trading Strategies Under Transaction Cost*, reports a prediction/net-return disconnect in hourly BTC and argues that transaction-cost-aware filtering can matter more than deeper architectures; it also notes uneven single-path returns and weak statistical significance.
- Bysik & Ślepaczuk (2026), *Machine Learning-Based Bitcoin Trading Under Transaction Costs*, finds naive hourly BTC strategies fail at 10 bps in tested configurations and that cost-aware forecast filtering can rescue selected strategies, without formal statistical dominance of XGBoost over neural alternatives.
