## New hypothesis
### H44 — Event-definition invariance
A candidate trading edge is credible only if the economic conclusion remains directionally consistent when the supervised label horizon and executable holding/event horizon are made explicitly consistent. If apparent alpha exists only under a mismatched label/execution definition, classify it as an implementation artifact rather than predictive alpha.
