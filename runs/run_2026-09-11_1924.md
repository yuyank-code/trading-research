   - `cost_stress` inside `outputs/final_model_report.json`: deterministic 0.5x/1x/1.5x/2x/3x fee+slippage results for each frozen OOS prediction set.

## New hypothesis

### H50 — Reproducible cost-survival and complete trial accounting

A candidate is not promotion-eligible unless the same frozen OOS predictions have:
- a complete trial-registry entry,
- deterministic cost-stress results over 0.5x–3x baseline costs,
- explicit execution assumptions,
- and a conclusion that does not depend on selecting the most favorable cost cell or configuration.

## Literature update

Recent 2026 evidence remains strongly aligned with this gate. Bysik & Ślepaczuk find naive hourly BTC ML strategies fail at 10 bps while cost-aware filtering rescues only selected configurations and does not establish formal model dominance.
