## Literature findings
1. Bailey & López de Prado, *The Deflated Sharpe Ratio* (Journal of Portfolio Management, 2014): selected Sharpe ratios are upward biased by multiple testing and non-normality. DSR should be applied after the full research trial count is frozen/reported.
2. Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu, *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting* (Journal of Computational Finance, 2015/2017): conventional holdouts are unreliable for investment backtests; CSCV/PBO directly evaluates whether the selected configuration is likely overfit.
3. Huang, Wang & Jiang (2026), *Research on Machine Learning High-Frequency Trading Strategies Under Transaction Cost*: hourly crypto experiments report a large prediction-to-trading gap and emphasize explicit fees, spread and slippage plus cost-aware filtering.
4. Bysik & Ślepaczuk (2026), *Machine Learning-Based Bitcoin Trading Under Transaction Costs: Evidence From Walk-Forward Forecasting*: reports that naive sign-based BTC ML strategies can fail under 10 bps costs, while forecast-magnitude filtering can reduce turnover; the paper also reports weak evidence for formal dominance of complex models.

## New testable hypotheses
### H15 — Reproducible-data sufficiency
A model result is promotable only if the exact market-data snapshot, feature-generation code/version, split specification, transaction-cost assumptions, and frozen predictions are all archived and sufficient for independent rerun.

### H16 — Cost-threshold monotonicity
For a fixed frozen signal, increasing round-trip execution cost should weakly reduce net performance after accounting for any endogenous trade filtering. A claimed edge that improves materially under harsher costs without an explicitly predeclared mechanism is an implementation red flag.

### H17 — Signal-strength monotonicity
If forecast magnitude is economically meaningful, conditional realized returns should increase with predeclared signal-strength bins out of sample. The bins must be fixed before the evaluation period; otherwise this becomes another selection trial.
