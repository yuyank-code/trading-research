# H90 — Selection-Aware Null Separation

**Status:** proposed, not tested

## Claim
A candidate discovered by the full research workflow should separate from the best candidate generated by an equally large, dependence-matched zero-alpha search after multiple-testing correction and realistic execution costs.

## Primary test
Compare the real-search winner with the distribution of winners from matched null searches using the same search budget, candidate families, seeds, purging/embargo, cost model and confirmation rules.

## Pass criteria
- real winner exceeds the null winner distribution by a pre-registered margin;
- DSR/PSR and PBO remain admissible after accounting for effective trial count;
- SPA/Reality Check does not reject the real candidate as indistinguishable from the benchmark/null;
- result survives adverse cost stress and major chronological subperiods;
- no structural leakage or forward-label overlap is detected.

## Non-pass
If the real winner is statistically consistent with the null winner distribution, classify the discovery as search luck / insufficient evidence and do not promote it.
